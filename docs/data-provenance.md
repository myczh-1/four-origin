# Data provenance

V2: six controlled fact types (number, owner name, configuration, synthetic path, date, boolean), seed 271828; train/validation/test/cross-template = 2000/300/500/200 pairs. Each pair has identical text with EXTERNAL/SELF identity switched; entities and full facts are disjoint across splits, but value vocabularies overlap. V2 training seed 2026; only 100 training pairs used in the archived run.

V3: two supplied atoms per record, mixed/conflicting/externally uncertain cases; data/order seed 31019 and adapter initialization seed 2026. Train/validation/transfer/test/cross-template = 320/24/12/48/48 pairs. Actual training = 200 pairs/400 records per adapter. Entity identifiers are disjoint but vocabulary and relation types are shared. Targets are generated by target(), not external human annotations. SELF inputs are constructed candidates, not authentic multistep inference.

Generated project identifiers, owners, dates and /srv/... paths are synthetic. No real repository, personal corpus or external benchmark was collected for V2/V3. Original answers, scores and the V3 limited manual review are included as historical artifacts, not independent human gold. No V4 trajectories or V4.1 annotations are republished here; only status disclosures.

Generators reside in experiments/origin_counterfactual_v2/run_experiment.py and experiments/origin_generation_v3/run_v3.py. Dataset bytes and archived hashes are in evidence/*/data and evidence/*/results.json. scripts/smoke.py verifies regeneration without any model forward pass.
